QUICK ANSWERS

Questions people ask before closeout.

Does it read my files?

It reads only the single folder you select, locally on your computer. Findings, cited paths, and short excerpts are returned to Claude and may be processed by Anthropic under your account settings. Only use documents you are authorized to share with Claude.

Does “found” mean the package is complete?

No. The result is a public-reference baseline. Your contract, specifications, owner requirements, and human review control.

Why are dates flagged?

Date-required baseline rows need a valid calendar date near a date label in the same readable source. A year alone is not enough.
