Closeout Readiness Check reviews one project folder you select, locally, on your Windows computer. M2 AI does not operate a project-document upload service for this product.

Project content stays local

Project filenames, paths, and extracted content from the selected folder stay on your computer. This product does not send that material to M2 AI, Stripe, Anthropic, or any model API, and it does not use OCR, telemetry, analytics, or advertising on your project files.

What the hosted service receives

The hosted service receives only minimal signed installation and entitlement data needed to activate a free check, validate a subscription, open the billing portal, or recover access. That includes an activation identifier, installation public key, mode, request purpose, timestamp, nonce, and signature, or an optional recovery key. It does not include project content. M2 AI does not operate a separate customer-account database for this product; entitlement metadata is associated with the Stripe subscription.

Payment information

Stripe processes payments. Stripe collects and stores the billing details entered at Checkout, typically including name, email, billing address, and payment-card information. M2 AI may see billing and subscription information in its Stripe account but does not receive or store a full card number.

Website and service metadata

Vercel hosts the site and activation endpoints and processes ordinary request data such as IP address, request time, and browser type to deliver and protect the service. M2 AI's pages use no analytics or advertising pixels. Stripe Checkout and the Stripe customer portal are Stripe pages governed by Stripe's privacy notice.

Purposes, disclosure, and retention

M2 AI uses billing and entitlement information only to provide the subscription, verify access, provide support, prevent abuse, and process cancellations or refunds. M2 AI discloses it to Stripe and Vercel only as needed to operate those services or when legally required. Billing records remain subject to Stripe's retention practices and applicable recordkeeping duties. Entitlement metadata remains associated with the subscription while needed to provide or document access. Local activation data remains on the computer until the user removes it.

Security and support

Entitlement requests are signed and purpose-bound. Do not email project documents, recovery keys, card details, or private keys. Support messages are kept only as reasonably needed to answer and document the issue.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask what personal information M2 AI holds, correct it, obtain a copy, or request deletion, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements. Email rmach816@outlook.com. M2 AI does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

Children

This business product is not directed to children under 13, and M2 AI does not knowingly collect their information through it.

Changes and contact

M2 AI may update this notice. A material change will be posted here before it takes effect. Questions: rmach816@outlook.com.
